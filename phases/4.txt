# MM Electricals — Phase 4

## Analytics, Business Intelligence & Owner Dashboard

The MM Electricals application now has:

* products
* vendors
* customers
* batches
* inventory
* purchases
* sales
* payments
* expenses
* pricing history

Do not rewrite existing functionality.

This phase focuses on:

**ANALYTICS AND OWNER INTELLIGENCE**

---

# 1. ANALYTICS PAGE

Create a dedicated premium Analytics page.

Do not simply put a few charts on the dashboard.

Analytics should provide actionable business information.

---

# 2. SALES ANALYTICS

Show:

* daily sales
* weekly sales
* monthly sales
* yearly sales
* sales growth
* average bill value
* number of invoices
* top-selling products
* top-selling brands
* top-selling categories
* top customers

Allow:

* date range
* category filter
* brand filter
* vendor filter
* customer filter

---

# 3. PROFIT ANALYTICS

Show:

* gross sales
* discounts
* cost of goods
* gross profit
* gross margin %
* profit by product
* profit by category
* profit by brand

Important:

Use actual historical batch cost where appropriate.

Do not simply calculate:

sales - current product cost

because historical costs can differ between batches.

---

# 4. INVENTORY ANALYTICS

Show:

* current stock quantity
* stock valuation
* low stock
* out of stock
* fast-moving products
* slow-moving products
* dead stock
* stock age
* stock turnover

Allow drill-down into products.

---

# 5. PURCHASE ANALYTICS

Show:

* total purchases
* purchase trend
* spending by vendor
* spending by category
* spending by brand
* most purchased products
* purchase frequency

---

# 6. VENDOR ANALYTICS

For each vendor show:

* total purchases
* number of products supplied
* average purchase price
* best historical price
* last purchase price
* purchase frequency
* outstanding amount

Allow product-level vendor comparison.

Example:

Product: 2.5 sq mm wire

Vendor A:
Average ₹1,500
Best ₹1,450

Vendor B:
Average ₹1,530
Best ₹1,480

Vendor C:
Average ₹1,510
Best ₹1,460

---

# 7. PRICE HISTORY ANALYTICS

For each product show purchase price history.

Example:

₹1,450 → ₹1,480 → ₹1,520 → ₹1,580

Show:

* absolute increase
* percentage increase
* vendor responsible
* date
* batch

This should help the owner understand supplier price trends.

---

# 8. BATCH ANALYTICS

Show:

* active batches
* remaining quantity per batch
* batch cost
* batch selling price
* safe selling price
* margin
* age of stock

If multiple batches exist for a product, show a comparison.

---

# 9. DISCOUNT ANALYTICS

Track:

* average discount given
* discount by product
* discount by customer type
* discount by customer
* discount impact on profit
* number of safe-price overrides

Identify products where excessive discounts are reducing margin.

---

# 10. CUSTOMER ANALYTICS

Show:

* top customers
* customer purchase value
* outstanding
* payment behavior
* credit utilization
* customer type performance

---

# 11. EXPENSE ANALYTICS

Show:

* total expenses
* expense by category
* monthly expense trend
* largest expense categories

---

# 12. DASHBOARD

Create a concise owner dashboard.

Prioritize:

Today's sales
Today's profit
Today's purchases
Outstanding receivables
Outstanding payables
Current stock value
Low stock
Top products
Recent sales
Important price alerts

Avoid visual clutter.

---

# 13. ALERTS / INSIGHTS

Create an extensible insight system.

Examples:

"2.5 sq mm wire purchase cost increased 9%."

"Polycab products generated 24% of gross profit this month."

"12 products are below reorder level."

"₹38,000 is outstanding from customers."

"15 products have had no sales in 90 days."

"Vendor ABC is currently offering the lowest recorded price for 2.5 sq mm wire."

Do not make unsupported predictions.

Insights must be derived from actual stored data.

---

# 14. EXPORT

Analytics and reports should support:

* CSV export
* print
* PDF where appropriate

---

# 15. PERFORMANCE

Analytics must not cause the application to become slow.

Use:

* optimized queries
* aggregation
* indexes
* pagination
* caching where appropriate

Do not calculate large datasets repeatedly in React.

---

# 16. TESTING

Create tests for:

* sales totals
* profit calculations
* batch cost calculations
* vendor comparisons
* inventory valuation
* customer balances
* discount analytics
* date filters

Verify analytics against known sample data.

At the end provide:

* analytics architecture
* calculation definitions
* queries/aggregation strategy
* tests
* known limitations
* Phase 5 production checklist
